# Relationship OS - Product Requirements Document

## Product

Relationship OS is an AI-powered personal relationship management application.

It helps users strengthen relationships with:

- Family
- Friends
- Coworkers
- Clients
- Mentors
- Business partners

Unlike Salesforce or HubSpot, this is NOT a sales CRM.

Its purpose is to help people maintain meaningful relationships.

---

## Target Users

- Professionals
- Entrepreneurs
- Students
- Freelancers
- Anyone wanting to remember important people

---

## Core Problem

People forget:

- Birthdays
- Promises
- Follow-ups
- Last conversations
- Important preferences

Relationship OS solves this.

---
